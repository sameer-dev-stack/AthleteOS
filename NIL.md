You are a senior product designer, elite landing-page copywriter, and full-stack frontend engineer.



I want you to design and build a premium, modern, conversion-focused landing page for a startup in the NIL (Name, Image, and Likeness) space.



The product idea:

We are building a platform similar in spirit to a “Me Card” for athletes — a digital athlete identity card + personal monetization page + AI-enhanced NIL growth platform.

Each athlete gets a public profile/card they can share with fans, brands, schools, and sponsors.

The platform helps athletes build their personal brand, look more professional, get discovered, and make money from one page.



Core product concept:

- Public athlete card/profile

- Athlete bio, sport, school, stats, highlights, links, media

- Monetization options like tips, bookings, paid shoutouts, memberships, affiliate links, merch, and sponsor interest

- AI tools that help athletes generate bios, captions, sponsor pitches, and profile improvements

- Free plan with strict AI limits

- Premium plan with more AI usage, analytics, customization, and monetization tools

- Paywall/upgrade system so free users are limited and power users pay



Business goal of landing page:

The page should make visitors immediately understand the product and push them toward joining a waitlist, requesting early access, or starting as an athlete.

This is not a generic sports website. It is a startup landing page for a scalable SaaS-style NIL platform.



Target users:

1. Student-athletes who want to build their personal brand

2. Athletes who want NIL deals and more visibility

3. Fans/supporters who want to support athletes

4. Brands/sponsors looking for athlete partnerships

5. Schools, collectives, or teams that may later use the platform at scale



Main positioning:

“Your NIL identity, monetization, and AI toolkit — all in one card.”



What to communicate:

- Athletes can share one beautiful page/card everywhere

- The page helps them look professional and become more discoverable

- The platform helps them monetize their audience and NIL opportunities

- AI makes setup faster and smarter

- Free tier exists, but advanced features are premium

- This is the easiest way to turn an athlete profile into a business asset



Tone:

- Bold, modern, premium, startup-grade

- Clear, punchy, investor-friendly

Emphasize:

- Generate pitch messages
